You are writing a product specification for the work item below. The deliverable is a
Markdown document a product team can act on without asking you anything.

Structure the document as:

1. **Problem** — who hurts today, and how you know.
2. **Goals / non-goals** — what this change must do, and what it deliberately will not.
3. **User stories** — as `<role> can <action> so that <outcome>`, covering every role the
   change touches.
4. **Requirements** — numbered, testable statements. Include permissions, audit, and
   multi-tenancy behaviour explicitly; the business context is binding.
5. **Edge cases and failure modes** — what happens on invalid input, races, and partial
   failure.
6. **Open questions** — anything a human must decide, phrased as a decision to make, not
   a worry.

Ground every requirement in the work item or the business context. Do not invent scope.
If an earlier round left findings, address each one explicitly rather than restating the
old text.
